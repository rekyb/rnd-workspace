Flagged assumptions

  • Mapel lists are extrapolated — no research source covers them. PAUD uses the six aspek perkembangan rather than subjects. Needs a native-speaker educator review.
  • The anonymised example follows jenjang (SD → “kelas 4”, SMA → “kelas 11”). Nothing in onboarding.md personalises it — this is our extrapolation.
  • M1 is shown as completed. The IA lists it first; the journey names “Prompt pertama Anda” as the landing’s active node. For M2 to be active, M1 must be done — but the docs never say onboarding completes M1, and onboarding is explicitly forbidden from carrying a tutorial. Its state is unresolved, not its existence.
  • .opt is a declared variant. DESIGN.md’s surface map specifies chip + bottom sheet for jenjang. Five options with hint text won’t sit in chips, and a sheet inside a boundary flow fights “one decision per screen”. If this stands, promote .opt into DESIGN.md as a named variant.
  • .ctxbar (the jenjang/mapel chips on the landing) is not in the surface map. It exists to make the context-carry visible; it is invented.
  • Back navigation is not in onboarding.md; added for gate G4.

Deliberately absent

The account moment — it fires after the first win, inside Belajar, not in the onboarding boundary.

The tab bar during onboarding — onboarding is a boundary flow.

M5–M7 and the readiness check — the path is truncated to a landing proof; Belajar has no screen-level design yet.
