## Goal
Audit three Brilliant features against expert heuristics and surface the build risks
and open questions each one raises: the AI conversational course recommender, the Koji
in-lesson learning companion, and non-MCQ interactive practice.

## Scope
- In scope: expert heuristic review of the three features named above, using captured
  stills of each surface.
- Out of scope: validated user research. `notes.md` states plainly that this is a
  hypothesis generator and risk register, not evidence, and tags each claim with a
  confidence level.

## Structure note
This study predates the current folder convention, so it does not follow the standard
benchmark layout. There is no `PLAN.md`, no `platforms/` tree, and no `SYNTHESIS.md`.
The complete write-up lives in `notes.md`, and its evidence is the seven captures in
`assets/` (two per feature, three for smart search). Treat `notes.md` as this study's
synthesis when reading the research board.
