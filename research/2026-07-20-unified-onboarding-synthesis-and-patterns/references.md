# References & External Validation

The following scholarly literature and industry standards were retrieved by the Domain Expert during the peer-review debate to corroborate or challenge the synthesized findings:

- **1EdTech (formerly IMS Global).** Verifiable Digital Credentials and Learning and Employment Records. https://www.1edtech.org
  - *Context:* Used to challenge the universal applicability of deferred registration, establishing that job-readiness platforms require verified identity.
- **Thaler, R. (1980).** "Toward a positive theory of consumer choice." *Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization*.
  - *Context:* Clarifies that the Endowment Effect requires a sense of psychological ownership, challenging the conflation with effort-based sunk costs.
- **Arkes, H. R., & Blumer, C. (1985).** "The psychology of sunk cost." *Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes*.
  - *Context:* Explains that time invested leads to the sunk cost fallacy, a weaker behavioral driver than true endowment.
- **Sweller, J. (1988).** "Cognitive load during problem solving: Effects on learning." *Cognitive Science*.
  - *Context:* Validates the "winnable-yet-credible" tension as a function of managing intrinsic vs extraneous cognitive load.
- **Csikszentmihalyi, M. (1990).** *Flow: The psychology of optimal experience*.
  - *Context:* Provides the framework for balancing challenge and skill in educational simulations.
- **Plass, J. L., et al. (1998).** "Cognitive load in reading a foreign language text with multimedia aids." *Computers in Human Behavior*.
  - *Context:* Validates that interacting with non-native UI imposes high extraneous cognitive load, requiring localization.
