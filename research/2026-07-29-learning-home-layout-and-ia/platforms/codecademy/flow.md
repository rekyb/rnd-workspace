# Flow — Codecademy dashboard (web)

**Summary:** the learner lands on `Dashboard`, reached through a **two-tier navigation**: a
global top bar for the catalogue, the company, and the account, and a left sidebar for the
learner's own work. Twelve destinations exist, and the split between them follows a rule.

Layout study. System-response claims **inferred from screen sequence**.

**1. Arrival — two navigation tiers, stacked.**
A **top bar** carries the wordmark and six items: `My Home`, `Catalog ▾`, `Resources ▾`,
`Community ▾`, `Live Learning` (with a `New` badge), `For Business`. Three of the six are
dropdowns. A right cluster holds search, notifications, and an avatar.

A **left sidebar** carries six more: `Dashboard` (active, marked by a filled row and a left
edge bar), `My learning`, `Skills tracking` (`New` badge), `Events`, `Projects`, `Workspaces`.
*Evidence: reference 01, 02, 03, 04.*

**2. A promotional bar sits above the entire application.**
A full-width yellow strip with a live countdown (`01d : 23h : 56m : 51s`), a discount claim,
an `Explore Bootcamps` button, and a dismiss `✕`. It sits above the top bar, displacing the
whole interface downward.
*Evidence: reference 01, 02, 04.* Reference 03 shows the app with the strip dismissed, so it
is dismissible.

**3. The first content block is promotional, not the learner's work.**
`Codecademy bootcamps` — a carousel with prev/next arrows holding a paid-programme card
(`Practical UI/UX Design with Figma for Beginners`, `Register now →`). The learner's own
progress appears **below** it.
*Evidence: reference 01, 02.*

**4. `Keep learning` carries the resume action, with three competing controls.**
A card headed by the path name (`Career path — Front-End Engineer`), a thin progress bar
labelled `1%`, and `Current Module: Getting Started`. Its footer is a three-way split:
`View path`, `Start practice session 0/1 today`, and a filled `Resume →`. Only `Resume` is
colour-filled. Below the card: `View all learning in progress ›`.
*Evidence: reference 01.*

**5. A second dashboard variant leads with topic selection instead.**
`Start learning` — a topic label (`Web Design, beginner-friendly`) with an `Edit ✏` control and
`Related topics: HTML & CSS`, then three course cards tagged `Free course`, `Skill path`, and
`Free course`, each carrying duration and difficulty.
*Evidence: reference 02.*

**6. The sidebar's foot holds a persistent promotional card.**
`Explore job opportunities` with a `Get started` button and a `Close` link (reference 01, 02,
04), replaced in another variant by `Try Plus or Pro with a 7-day free trial` and a `Try for
free` button (reference 02). It occupies the sidebar below the six nav items.

**7. `My learning` and `Projects` reuse the same shell.**
Both keep both nav tiers and swap only the centre column. `My learning` presents
`In progress` / `Completed` tabs, a `Self-enrolled` group, and expandable course rows showing
per-item checkmarks (`Lesson`, `Project`, `Quiz`, `Informational`) with a
`Start module practice` button and a `4 / 9 concepts practiced` counter. `Projects` at zero
shows a centred `Ready for a new project?` with a single `Find next project` button.
*Evidence: reference 03, 04.*

---

**Not observed:** what sits inside the three top-bar dropdowns, any narrow-width behaviour,
and whether the countdown is genuine or perpetual.
