# Flow — Coursera home (web)

**Summary:** the learner lands on `Home`, reached through **four learner tabs sitting under a
global utility bar**. The centre column leads with the resume action; a companion card carries
the weekly goal. Everything below the learner's own work is catalogue merchandising.

Layout study. System-response claims **inferred from screen sequence**.

**1. Arrival — two rows, with different jobs.**
Row one, the **global bar**: wordmark, an `Explore ▾` button, a wide search field with a
submit control, a cart icon with a badge, a `🌐 English ▾` selector, notifications, and an
avatar `▾`. Row two, the **learner tabs**: `Home`, `My Learning`, `Online Degrees`, `Careers` —
four items, with `Home` underlined as the active state.
*Evidence: reference 01, 02, 03.*

**2. The centre opens with the resume action, headed by section.**
`Continue learning` with a `View all learning` link on the right. Below it a provider label
(`Google UX Design`), then a wide card split into two panes: left carries the course name
(`Foundations of User Experience (UX) Design`), `Course 1 of 7 · 8% complete`, and a thin
progress bar; right carries the next item (`Welcome to module 1`, `Video (1 minute)`) and a
filled blue `Resume` button, with an overflow `…`.
*Evidence: reference 01, 02.*

**3. A companion card carries the weekly goal, beside the resume card.**
`Weekly goal progress tracker`, a sentence stating the commitment (`I'm committed to learning
3 days a week on Coursera`), seven weekday circles with completed days filled, and an
`Edit my goal` link.
*Evidence: reference 01, 02.*

**4. A full-width strip restates the career goal and offers to change it.**
`Your career goal is to start a career as a Product Designer` with an `Edit goal` link. A
single low-height row, visually quieter than the cards above it.
*Evidence: reference 01, 02.*

**5. Below the learner's own work, the page turns to merchandising.**
Two variants captured: `Recently Viewed Products` (course cards with provider logos, `New AI
skills` badges, `Build toward a degree`, `Professional Certificate`) and `Earn Your Degree`
(university cards, each a campus photograph and an institution name).
*Evidence: reference 01, 02.*

**6. `My Learning` reuses the shell and moves the goal cards into a rail.**
The centre becomes a heading (`My Learning`), `In Progress` / `Completed` chips, and a vertical
course list — each row a course name and `Course n of 7 · Not started`, with the active row
expanded to show the next item and a `Resume` button. A **right rail** appears holding a
greeting card (`Good afternoon, Alex Smith`, the career goal restated, `Edit goal`) and the
`Weekly goal progress tracker` card.
*Inferred from screen sequence — reference 01 → 03.*

**Not observed:** the contents of `Explore ▾`, narrow-width behaviour, and whether the rail
persists on `Online Degrees` or `Careers`.
