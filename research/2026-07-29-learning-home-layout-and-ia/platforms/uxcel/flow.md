# Flow — Uxcel home (web)

**Summary:** the learner lands on `Home`, reached through a **grouped left sidebar** carrying
thirteen destinations under two labelled headings. The centre column holds the resume action
and recommendations; a persistent right rail holds streak, league, and offers.

Layout study. System-response claims **inferred from screen sequence**.

**1. Arrival — thirteen destinations in three clusters.**
The sidebar opens with the wordmark and a search field, then:

- **Ungrouped, top:** `Home` (active, filled pill), `Bookmarks`, `Leagues`
- **`LEARN`** (label in small caps): `Courses`, `Career Paths`, `Briefs`, `Assessments`, `Tutorials`, `Arcade`
- **`GROW`** (label in small caps): `Showcase`, `Certifications`, `Salary Explorer`, `Job Board`

A top-right cluster holds `Upgrade`, a lightning counter, a points counter, `Earn $50`,
notifications, and an avatar.
*Evidence: reference 01, 02, 03.*

**2. The centre column opens with the resume action.**
`Continue learning` as a section heading, then a wide course card: thumbnail, `COURSE` label,
course name (`Core UI Components`), `Current Lesson: The Anatomy of UI Components`, a thin
progress bar reading `6%`, a `7h left` estimate, and a filled `Resume course` button.
*Evidence: reference 01.*

**3. Recommendations follow, as a second section.**
`Recommended for you` with a `View all` link, then course cards each carrying an illustration,
a `COURSE` label, a title, an author name and avatar, and a bookmark control.
*Evidence: reference 01.*

**4. The right rail stacks three cards, in a fixed order.**
Top: a dismissible offer (`Bring Uxcel to your team`, `Learn more`, `book a demo`) or an
`Individual` / `Team` toggle with `Get full access with Pro membership` and `Upgrade now`.
Middle: a streak card (`2 day streak` or `0 day streak`) with seven weekday dots, filled for
completed days, and a lightning glyph. Bottom: a `Quartz league` card — `Top 20 advance`,
`4 days left`, and a ranked list of members with points, the learner's own row highlighted.
*Evidence: reference 01, 02.*

**5. A zero-course home replaces the resume card and adds a checklist to the rail.**
The centre becomes `Continue learning — You don't have any active courses`. The rail gains a
`Getting started` card: `Unlock your Aha! moment by working through these quick, rewarding
steps`, listing `Start your first course`, `Complete a lesson quiz`, `Take Uxcel Pulse test`,
each with a check control. A `Bulletin board` section with a count badge appears in the centre
below, holding cards such as `Take the Uxcel career quiz` with duration and question count.
*Evidence: reference 02.* **Cited for block order and rail composition only; zero-state
composition is settled by `2026-07-28` F2.*

**6. Scrolling reveals events, then a resources grid, then the page ends.**
Event cards (`Workshop`, `Portfolio Reviews`, each with audience, duration, and date), then a
`Resources` section: a six-cell grid of external destinations — `Mobile App`, `Slack App`,
`Figma Templates`, `Notion Templates`, `Discord Community`, `Reddit Community`, each an icon,
a title, and one line of description.
*Evidence: reference 03.*

---

**Not observed:** narrow-width behaviour, what the sidebar does when the viewport shrinks, and
whether the rail is persistent across non-`Home` destinations.
