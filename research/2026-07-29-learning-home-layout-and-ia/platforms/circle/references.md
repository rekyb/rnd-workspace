## Why this platform was captured

`PLAN.md` set a checkable trigger: capture Circle **only if fewer than two of the five learning
platforms show a labelled nav group.** Evaluated after the six learning captures:

| Platform | Labelled nav group? |
|---|---|
| Uxcel | **Yes** — `LEARN`, `GROW` |
| Codecademy | No — two tiers, neither carrying a group label |
| Duolingo | No |
| Babbel | No |
| Coursera | No |
| Mimo / Speak (iOS) | No — flat tab bars |

**One instance, not two. Trigger fired; Circle captured.** Without it, the study's grouping
evidence would rest on a single platform, and Q1's four-way typology would have an unsupported
arm.

**Standing:** control case, not a learning product. An audience-transfer caveat rides on every
finding it supports, and it may not be the sole source of a design implication.
