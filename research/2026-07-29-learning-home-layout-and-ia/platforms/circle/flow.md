# Flow — Circle authenticated home (web)

**Summary:** a community platform whose sidebar carries **fourteen destinations under three
labelled group headings**, plus a top nav of five. Captured as the non-education control for
Q1 grouping.

Layout study, single screen. System-response claims **inferred from a single still** — weaker
than elsewhere in this study, and treated accordingly.

**1. Arrival — two navs, one grouped.**
A **top bar** carries a workspace switcher and five items: `Home`, `Courses`, `Events`,
`Members`, `Leaderboard`, with `Home` in an active pill. To its right, search, notifications,
bookmarks, and an avatar.

A **left sidebar** carries fourteen destinations under three labelled headings:

- **Ungrouped, top:** `Getting Started`, `Feed`
- **`Get Started`:** `Start Here` (active, filled), `Say Hello`, `Share Resources`
- **`Product`:** `Give Feedback`, `Ask for Help`, `Announcements`, `Knowledge Base`
- **`Spaces`:** `UX Meet-Ups`, `Learn Everyday`, `Chit-chats`, `Know Your Friends`, `Images`

The sidebar's foot holds a `Go live` button and a `Powered by Circle` line.
*Evidence: reference 01.*

**2. A trial banner spans the content area.**
A dark full-width strip: `Your trial ends in 8 days on 10/11. Upgrade now and take advantage of
our annual discounts with up to 20% off!`
*Evidence: reference 01.*

**3. The centre column is a feed, headed by the active space.**
A title row (`🎉 Start Here`) with a `Latest ▾` sort control, an avatar stack, a filled
`New post` button, and an overflow `…`. Below it a secondary row (`Important Link`, `Folder ▾`,
`+`), then a composer (`Start a post` with a `+`), then post cards each carrying an author
avatar, title, relative timestamp, and reaction and comment counts.
*Evidence: reference 01.*

**4. A right rail stacks two cards.**
`Get started` — a three-item checklist (`Basics`, `Setup a paywall`, `Invite your first
members`), each with a green check. Below it `Members` — a list of avatars with names, and a
`See members` link.
*Evidence: reference 01.*

**5. A toast and a floating control sit over the layout.**
A dark `Changes saved.` toast is centred near the foot; a circular chat control floats at the
lower right.
*Evidence: reference 01.*

---

**Not observed:** every other destination, any narrow-width behaviour, and whether the sidebar
groups are collapsible (the `Get Started`, `Product`, and `Spaces` headings appear to carry
disclosure affordances, but this is **not asserted from one still**).
